# Project Overview

## Background and Motivation

The idea for this project emerged from a real-world problem. Most hospitality businesses still prepare their weekly employee schedules manually. This creates several challenges when planning weekly shifts and ensuring that all working hours are adequately covered.

Such businesses often employ a large number of student workers, whose availability cannot be restricted to fixed working hours. Flexible working schedules are also commonly offered as an employment benefit to attract new employees. As a result, managers face the challenge of preparing weekly schedules that cover all required working hours while also accommodating employees' individual availability and preferences.

In the client's business, this process is currently handled using a simple paper-based system. Managers have to review messages received through various communication channels, including verbal requests, SMS messages, and Messenger conversations, and combine all the collected information into a single schedule that works for everyone.

Once the schedule is prepared, the final version must be shared with all employees, along with any subsequent changes. This entire process requires a considerable amount of time, effort, and organizational work.

To simplify and improve this routine process, we decided to develop **Shifty**, a shift management application designed to address the challenges described above.

## Project Goals

The main goal of Shifty is to simplify employee scheduling and improve communication between managers and employees in hospitality businesses.

The application will provide a centralized platform for managing work shifts, collecting employee availability, handling shift exchange requests, and tracking working hours.

By automating and organizing these processes, Shifty aims to reduce the administrative workload of managers, improve scheduling efficiency, and provide employees with a more convenient and transparent way to manage their work schedules.

The application will be designed to accommodate the flexible working arrangements commonly found in the hospitality industry, particularly when employing student workers.
